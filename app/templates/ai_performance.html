Known limits

{{ icon('bell') }}
Detailed everyday notes can look like a rare work-up. When a note lists several investigations (CT, cultures, ECG), the realist may say "rule out common causes too"; the common conditions then appear below the rare list. Scroll down for them.
{{ icon('bell') }}
Rare diseases with mostly everyday symptoms are occasionally shown common causes first. If the case has specific signs, read the rare list too.
{{ icon('message') }}
Unusual wording may be missed. Ticking symptoms from the list helps the realist and the ranking.
{{ icon('check') }}
Clean, specific findings work best. Signs such as angioid streaks, a corneal copper ring or positive antibodies lift the right disease to the top, even in very short notes.
